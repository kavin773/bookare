FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Real questions from incoming international students moving to France.

Can I book accommodation and sign my French lease before landing in France? ▼
Yes, absolutely. 100% of Bookare listings are pre-verified so that international students can safely secure their accommodation and obtain the official French rental contract required for their VLS-TS student visa application before boarding their flight. Your deposit is safely held in escrow until 24 hours after you physically move in.
What is the Visale guarantee and is it really free for international students? ▼
Visale is a program financed by Action Logement (a French public institution) that acts as a legally certified French guarantor for students under 30 years old. It covers up to 36 months of unpaid rent. Any international student with a valid passport and student visa can apply online for 100% free. Bookare pre-checks your dossier to ensure 1-day approval.
How does the CAF (APL) housing allowance work in France? ▼
The Caisse d'Allocations Familiales (CAF) provides monthly housing aid (APL) directly to students living in eligible private apartments or residences in France. For a €460/month studio in Nantes, international students typically receive between €150 and €210 per month paid back into their French bank account. You can apply as soon as you have signed your lease.
What happens if my French student visa is delayed or denied? ▼
Under Bookare's Visa Protection Policy, if your student visa application is officially rejected by the French consulate or Campus France, your booking deposit and Bookare fee are 100% refunded when you submit the official rejection letter at least 48 hours prior to the lease start date.
What is an "État des lieux" and why is it so critical? ▼
An état des lieux is a legally binding inventory and condition report conducted together with the landlord on your move-in day. You inspect every wall, light, faucet, and piece of furniture and record any pre-existing marks. This document is what protects your security deposit from being deducted when you move out at the end of the year.
Do I need a French bank account to pay my rent? ▼
Your initial booking and deposit through Bookare can be paid with any international Visa, Mastercard, or foreign transfer. However, for monthly ongoing rent and to receive your CAF housing subsidy, French law requires a European SEPA bank account with a French IBAN/RIB. We partner with BNP Paribas and online banks so you can open an account smoothly upon arrival.
