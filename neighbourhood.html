The Vibe & Daily Lifestyle

Saint-Félix is nestled along the peaceful Erdre river and is directly adjacent to Audencia Atlantic Campus and Université de Nantes. It’s ideal for students who prioritize walking to 8:30 AM lectures, studying in quiet cafes, and jogging along the river trails on weekends.

🛒 Supermarkets: Carrefour City (bd Michelet), Super U, Bio c' Bon.
🥖 Local Spots: Boulangerie Saint-Félix, Le Bar de l'Erdre, University CROUS restaurants.
🚊 Transit: Tram Line 2 (Stops: Michelet-Sciences, Morrhonnière, Petit Port).

The Vibe & Daily Lifestyle

If you want to step out of your building right into charming medieval streets, French bistro terraces, international restaurants, and cinema theaters, Centre-Ville is unmatched. Tram Line 2 starts right at Commerce and runs directly north to Audencia.

🛒 Supermarkets: Monoprix, Fnac, Marché Talensac within 5 min.
🍕 Food & Drink: Rue Kervégan pubs, Place Royale cafes, Bouffay street food.
🚊 Transit: Commerce station — intersection of Tram 1, 2, 3 and major night buses.

The Vibe & Daily Lifestyle

Located just northwest of the centre, Hauts-Pavés offers the perfect balance: a peaceful residential environment with tree-lined streets, local cheese shops and wine cellars, yet only 10 minutes by bus or bike to Audencia.

🥐 Attractions: Marché Talensac (Nantes' famous covered fresh food market).
🚲 Biking: Direct protected cycleways along Bd des Anglais straight to campus.
🚊 Transit: Tram Line 3 (Félix Faure / Poitou) and Chronobus Line C2.

"If you want to avoid late-night bar noise but still want to cycle easily to both class and social dinners, Hauts-Pavés is the insider favourite among international master's students."

The Vibe & Daily Lifestyle

The former naval shipyards of Nantes have been transformed into a European creative district. Surrounded by the Loire river, it houses the National School of Architecture, Audencia Médiacampus, and modern student buildings with gyms and rooftop terraces.

🐘 Iconic: The Great Mechanical Elephant and the Hangar à Bananes music venues.
🏢 Residences: Newly constructed eco-residences with fiber internet and laundry hubs.
🚊 Transit: Busway Line 5 and Tram Line 1.

"Ideal for Audencia Médiacampus students or anyone who loves contemporary apartments with modern elevators and built-in kitchen appliances."
