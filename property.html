About this home

This bright and modern studio apartment is located in the quiet Orvault neighbourhood, just 2.1 km from the Audencia Business School campus. The apartment features large windows with natural light, a fully equipped kitchenette, and a private bathroom.

The accommodation is ideal for an international student arriving for the first time in Nantes. It is located near several supermarkets, a pharmacy and the Orvault tram stop. The landlord is welcoming of international students and has previously hosted students from over 20 countries.

The apartment is available from 1 September 2025. A minimum stay of 6 months is required.
