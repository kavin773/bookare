Campus Map & Transit

Audencia Campuses in Nantes

Audencia has 3 key campuses in Nantes. Make sure you book accommodation close to yours!

Main Campus

Atlantic Campus

📍 8 route de la Jonelière, 44312 Nantes

Home of the Grande École, MSc programs, and MBA. Next to École Centrale de Nantes and University campus.

Transit: Tram Line 2 (Stop: École Centrale - Audencia) or Bus 26 / 80.
City Centre

City Campus (Graslin)

📍 4 rue Bisson, 44100 Nantes

Executive education, bachelor programs, and incubator. Situated right in the historic Graslin shopping district.

Transit: Tram Line 1 (Stop: Médiathèque) or 5 min walk from Place Royale.
Creative District

Médiacampus

📍 41 Bd de la Prairie au Duc, Île de Nantes

Audencia SciencesCom (Communication & Media school) located on the modern Île de Nantes innovation island.

Transit: Busway Line 5 or 12 min walk from Tram Line 1 (Chantiers Navals).

Nantes Public Transit (Naolib / Tan)

Nantes has one of the best public transit networks in France. Tram Line 2 directly connects Nantes city centre (Commerce) to Audencia Atlantic Campus in just 18 minutes!

🚊 Unlimited Student Tan Pass: ~€28/month (under 26 years old) for all trams, buses, and river shuttles.
🚲 Bicloo City Bikes: Over 120 self-service bike stations with paths along the Erdre river to Audencia.
🎉 Weekend Free Transit: All trams and buses in Nantes are 100% free on Saturdays and Sundays!

Recommended Commute Radius for Audencia:

  • ✓ Saint-Félix: 10-15 min walk / 4 min bike (Top choice)
  • ✓ Michelet / Petit Port: 5-10 min walk (Closest)
  • ✓ Hauts-Pavés / Rond-Point de Rennes: 10 min bus / 8 min bike
  • ✓ Centre-Ville: 18 min direct tram 2
