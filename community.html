How does CAF work and how do I apply for housing benefit as a student?

I've heard CAF can reduce rent significantly. Is it available to international students? How do I apply and how long does it take?

Admin / CAF All cities 💬 21 answers ✓ Answered Posted by Priya L.
✓ Best answer (by Rahul Sharma, Audencia)

Yes! CAF is available to international students on a valid visa. You apply at caf.fr after you receive your first rent receipt. It usually takes 4–6 weeks. Most students get €80–€200/month. Start immediately when you arrive. You'll need your lease, passport, visa, RIB (bank details) and enrollment certificate.
